LiveAdsTools — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

LiveAdsTools ("the App") is an internal software tool operated by AURACOMES LLC ("we", "us") to create and manage Facebook advertising campaigns for our own business Pages and ad accounts. The App is not offered to the public and has no end-user login.

1. What data the App accesses

Through the Meta Graph API and Marketing API, using a System User token belonging to our own verified Business Manager, the App accesses only:

  • Our own Facebook Pages: Page IDs, Page names, live video and post identifiers;
  • Our own ad accounts: campaign, ad set, ad and creative objects, and their performance metrics (spend, reach, impressions, actions);
  • Our own pixel IDs for conversion tracking.

The App does not access, collect or store personal data of Facebook users, viewers, customers or any third party.

2. How we use the data

The data is used solely to create, review, activate, pause and report on advertising campaigns that promote our own livestream sales sessions. It is not used for any other purpose.

3. Storage and retention

Campaign, ad set, ad and creative identifiers created by the App, together with operation logs, are stored locally on our own computers for operational and audit purposes. Access tokens are stored in a local, access-restricted configuration file and are never transmitted to any third party.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, share, transfer or disclose any Platform Data to third parties. Data is exchanged only between the App and Meta's APIs.

5. Data deletion

Because the App stores no personal data of third parties, there is nothing to delete on request. Locally stored campaign identifiers and logs can be deleted at any time by our administrators. To request deletion of any data or to ask questions, contact us at the address below.

6. Security

The App runs on our own equipment, uses HTTPS for all API calls, and follows Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.
